Exam Prep AI

User Manual

Product guide · September 2026

Welcome

Exam Prep AI turns an exam name and state or jurisdiction into a structured study path. It combines an AI-generated exam overview, personalized study materials, a timed practice exam, and detailed feedback so you can move from orientation to focused practice in one session.

This manual explains what the app does, how to use each feature, and what to expect while content is being generated.

Important: Exam Prep AI is a study aid, not an official testing authority. AI-generated information can be incomplete or outdated. Always confirm eligibility, registration steps, fees, deadlines, passing requirements, and other high-stakes details with the official exam administrator or licensing agency.

What the app includes

1. Exam setup

Enter:

  • Exam name — for example, Real Estate License or a professional certification.
  • State or jurisdiction — for example, California.

The app uses both values to tailor its content. Select Start Studying to begin.

2. Exam overview

The overview organizes the information a candidate usually needs before studying:

  • Exam purpose and a short overview
  • Eligibility requirements
  • Question count and exam duration when available
  • Passing score information
  • Major exam sections and key topics
  • Registration process
  • State-specific notes
  • Exam-day tips

Use Generate Study Guide at the bottom of the page when you are ready to move from orientation into preparation.

3. Personalized study guide

The study guide is organized into four tabs:

  • Study Plan — a recommended approach and suggested resources.
  • High-Yield Notes — explanations of major topics, labeled by importance.
  • Flashcards — interactive question-and-answer cards. Select a card to flip it and reveal the answer.
  • Mnemonics — memory aids for rules, lists, processes, and other concepts.

The guide is generated for the exam and state you entered. It is designed to prioritize the material most likely to help you study efficiently.

4. Timed mock exam

Select Take Mock Exam from the study guide or Mock Exam in the top navigation.

The practice exam includes:

  • 20 multiple-choice questions by default
  • Four answer options per question
  • Questions grouped by exam topic or section
  • A progress indicator showing the current question and percentage answered
  • Previous and Next controls
  • A countdown timer when the generated exam provides a time limit
  • Automatic submission when the timer reaches zero

You can move between questions and change an answer before submitting. You must answer at least one question to submit manually. Unanswered questions are included in grading as unanswered.

5. Results and feedback

After submission, Exam Prep AI shows:

  • Percentage score
  • Pass or fail indication based on the generated practice threshold
  • Number of correct answers out of the total
  • Recommended topics to review
  • Per-question feedback
  • Your answer and the correct answer
  • An explanation for each question

From the results page, choose Review Study Guide to return to your materials or Retake Exam to generate another practice attempt.

How to use Exam Prep AI

Step 1: Start with a specific exam

Open the app home page and enter the full exam name. Include the credential or license name when possible so the generated content has useful context.

Example:

  • Exam name: California Real Estate Salesperson License
  • State: California

Select Start Studying.

Step 2: Review the overview before memorizing

Read the eligibility, format, passing score, registration, and state-specific sections first. This gives you a checklist of administrative requirements and a map of the subjects you will study.

Treat this page as a planning aid. Confirm official requirements before applying or booking an exam.

Step 3: Work through the study guide

Start with the Study Plan tab. Then use High-Yield Notes to learn the major concepts. Use Flashcards for retrieval practice and Mnemonics when you need help remembering sequences, rules, or lists.

A practical study cycle is:

  1. Read the plan and identify your available study time.
  2. Review high-importance notes first.
  3. Test recall with flashcards instead of rereading only.
  4. Use mnemonics for details that are easy to confuse.
  5. Take the mock exam under realistic conditions.

Step 4: Take the mock exam without looking at the guide

Read each question carefully and select one option. Use Previous if you want to revisit an earlier question. The progress bar counts answered questions, so use it to identify anything you may have skipped.

The timer is a practice constraint, not a guarantee of the official exam's time limit. Compare the app's practice settings with the official exam format.

Step 5: Turn results into a next study session

Start with the recommended focus areas, then read the explanations for incorrect answers. Return to the related notes, flashcards, or mnemonics before taking the exam again.

A passing practice score is encouraging, but it is not an official score or a guarantee of exam readiness.

What to expect while content is generated

Exam Prep AI creates content on demand. Typical waits are:

  • Exam overview: about 10–15 seconds
  • Study guide: about 20–30 seconds
  • Mock exam: about 15–20 seconds
  • Grading: wait for the grading screen to finish after submitting

Keep the app open while a request is processing. Loading screens explain what is being prepared. If a request fails, use Try Again. A failed request should not be treated as completed study material.

Navigation

After you enter an exam and state, the top navigation provides:

  • Overview — return to exam facts and planning information.
  • Study Guide — return to notes, flashcards, mnemonics, and the study plan.
  • Mock Exam — start another practice exam.

The header also shows the active exam and state so you can confirm that the generated content is for the intended target.

Best practices

  • Use the official exam name and the correct state or jurisdiction.
  • Verify legal, licensing, eligibility, and registration information independently.
  • Study the High-Yield Notes before relying on the flashcards.
  • Use active recall: answer a flashcard before flipping it.
  • Take the mock exam in one sitting when possible.
  • Review explanations for both correct and incorrect answers.
  • Treat pass/fail feedback as a practice signal, not an official determination.
  • Start a new session if you need to prepare for a different exam.

Troubleshooting

The app is still loading

AI generation can take up to about 30 seconds depending on the request. Keep the page open and wait for the result.

A page says generation failed

Select Try Again. Check that your exam name and state are correct. If the request repeatedly fails, verify your network connection and try again later.

I opened a deep link or refreshed the page

The active exam, generated mock exam, and results are held in the current app session. Refreshing or opening a study page without first setting up an exam may return you to the home page. Start the exam flow again if needed.

The information does not match my official source

Use the official exam administrator or licensing agency as the authority. The app's content is generated for study support and should be checked before you act on it.

My score looks unexpected

Review the detailed feedback and confirm that you selected the intended answer on each question. The practice result is generated from the submitted questions and answers and is meant to guide further study.

Support and feedback

When reporting a problem, include:

  1. The exam name and state you entered.
  2. The page where the problem occurred.
  3. Whether the problem happened during generation, the exam, submission, or results.
  4. The exact error message, if one appeared.
  5. Whether Try Again changed the outcome.

This information makes it easier to reproduce the issue without sharing private credentials or sensitive personal information.
